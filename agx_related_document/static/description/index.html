Related Documents

Lets a record cite other documents as supporting evidence (เอกสารอ้างอิง) — in-system ones picked from a dropdown or found by number, or out-of-system ones given by number and subject, each optionally with a file. A citation is evidence only: it never drives budget, dimensions, route or prefill.

A model opts in by inheriting related.document.mixin and showing related_document_ids with the embedded list view; the module adds no field to any other model.

  • Kinds (ชนิดเอกสารอ้างอิง, Settings ▸ Technical) are master data: a model, its number/subject/date fields, which models may cite it, a target domain, whether a typed number is also looked up under sudo, and whether the target gets a ถูกอ้างอิงโดย action-menu entry.
  • Rows keep a snapshot (number, date, subject, file). เปิดเอกสาร opens the target under the reader’s own rights.
  • Whoever reads the citing record reads its rows; who may change them is the citing model’s _related_document_check_change hook (default: write access). Every addition and removal is logged in its chatter.
  • Rows are never copied, die with the citing record, and never block deleting or cancelling either document.
