Ties a loan to the ใบจองงบประมาณ (``budget.commitment``) of its source
document (ใบขออนุมัติ / ใบขอให้จัดหา), without the loan holding a commitment
of its own (advance_payment ADR-0025, ADR-0029):

- **วงเงินยืมคงเหลือ**: the commitment's reserved amount minus every
  non-cancelled loan drawn on it. A loan may not exceed it.
- The source's commitment must be reserved or partly used.
- The disbursement voucher (ใบสำคัญจ่ายเงินยืม) takes the commitment
  header's financial dimensions.

Both conditions are borrowing rules (exception rules) the administrators
may switch off or change from the Exception Rules menu (ADR-0030).

Any source model with a ``budget_commitment_id`` field is supported; no
dependency on the source modules.
