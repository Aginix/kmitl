Adds a **Role** field (Academic / Support) to employees, allowing organizations
to classify staff by their functional role.

The role is visible and filterable in the employee form, list, and kanban views,
and is also exposed on the public employee model.

Usage
=====

1. Go to **Employees → Employees**.
2. Open an employee record and set the **Role** field to *Academic* or *Support*.
3. Use the **Role** filter or group-by option in the list/kanban view to organize employees by role.
