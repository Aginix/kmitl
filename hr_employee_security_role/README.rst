Splits the default Odoo HR **Officer** role into two tiers:

- **Department Officer** – can only view employees within the same
  master department.
- **Central Office Staff** – can manage (read/write/create/delete) all
  employees across departments.

The HR Manager role automatically inherits Central Office Staff
permissions. The module also restricts the chatter and HR configuration
menu to Central Office Staff and above.

Usage
=====

1. Go to **Settings → Users & Companies → Users**.
2. Assign the appropriate HR group:

   - **Officer: Manage all department employees** – department-scoped
     read access.
   - **Central Office Staff: Manage All employees** – full access to all
     employees.

3. Department officers will only see employees sharing their
   ``master_department_id``.
