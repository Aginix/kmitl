# Cross-OU reservation: a beneficiary OU sees the slip; spend counts against the funder's dimension

When one unit reserves budget *for* another (the support scenario: central reserves, a unit spends), the money **stays the funder's** — it is not transferred. So a standalone `budget.commitment` carries the funder's `operating_unit_id` (the **Owning Unit**) plus a new single Many2one **`beneficiary_operating_unit_id`** (the **Beneficiary Unit**, the requesting unit — one per slip). The OU `ir.rule` on **both** `budget.commitment` and `budget.commitment.line` is extended so a user sees the slip when their OU matches **either** the owner **or** the beneficiary (the line rule traverses `commitment_id.beneficiary_operating_unit_id`). Because the drawn document inherits the slip's dimensions locked, the spend's **financial `department` dimension is the funder's** (central) while the **OU** on the พ.1 / disbursement / `budget.move` is the **beneficiary's** (the operator): the money counts against central's budget, and per-unit spend of supported money is answered by an OU/beneficiary lens, never by a `department` report.

## Considered Options

- **Stamp the beneficiary's OU directly on `operating_unit_id`** (no new field, no rule change) — rejected: loses "who reserved it," and the requirement anticipates that the funder will not always be central (a unit may reserve for another unit); a distinct field keeps owner vs beneficiary explicit.
- **`operating_unit_id = False`** (visible to everyone) — rejected: not targeted; every unit would see a slip meant for one.
- **Many2many beneficiaries** — deferred: the current process is one beneficiary per slip; a single Many2one is sufficient and cheaper. Revisit only if pooled multi-unit reservations arise.

## Consequences

- New field `beneficiary_operating_unit_id` on `budget.commitment`, plus a two-rule change in `budget_operating_unit` (the rules gate **read only** — `perm_write=0` — so the beneficiary unit can create obligate/consume lines on a slip it does not own).
- This deliberately exercises the CONTEXT.md rule that **OU ≠ the `department` financial dimension**: OU is the operator/visibility axis; `department` is where the money lives.
- The `budget.move` produced by a beneficiary's consume carries **OU = beneficiary** (creator default) but **`department` = funder** (locked from the slip) — consistent, since OU is never summed in reports.
- Clearly distinct from a **Budget Transfer** (การโอนงบ, the other support case): a transfer *moves ownership* of the money, so the spend counts against the **receiving** unit's `department`; a reservation does not.

## Governance

- **Reserving *for another unit* is gated.** Setting `beneficiary_operating_unit_id` to an OU other than the owner (`operating_unit_id`) requires the `group_all_ou_budget` right (which central-planning already holds). A plain `budget_user` may reserve only for its own OU (beneficiary empty or equal to owner), keeping ordinary reservation low-friction while restricting the cross-OU *support* act to central-planning. The gate is an `@api.constrains` on `budget.commitment` that lives in **`budget_operating_unit`** — alongside the field and the visibility-widening rules, not in the optional `budget_operating_unit_access_all` — so it can never be bypassed by installing the base OU module without access-all; it looks the group up with `raise_if_not_found=False`, so when access-all is absent (the group does not exist) reserving for another unit is blocked outright (safe default).
- **Known gap, deliberately left open (layer 1).** Neither the availability engine (`budget.controller`) nor the reservation picker applies any OU/department *ownership* guard: `budget.account` has no OU/owner field and the engine ignores OU entirely, so any `budget_user` can already reserve against **any** department's pool simply by picking that department in the blank-slate picker. This is pre-existing and system-wide, not introduced by this feature. The beneficiary-gate therefore stops "reserving *for* someone else," **not** "reserving *from* another unit's pool for oneself." Closing it later would mean applying the existing analytic-OU soft-filter (`operating_unit_ids` tag on analytic accounts + `group_all_ou_analytic`) inside the picker (which currently loads dimensions via `orm.searchRead` and bypasses it); deferred by choice to keep this change minimal.
