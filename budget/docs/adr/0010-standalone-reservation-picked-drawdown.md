# Standalone budget reservation, picked and drawn down by consuming documents

A `budget.commitment` can now be created **directly** as a first-class ใบจองงบประมาณ (Budget Reservation) — reserved up front (`draft → reserved` via a "จองงบประมาณ" action; a `budget_user` may reserve immediately, no tier.validation) with **no originating source document** — generalising the shared-commitment pattern that previously only a `procurement.plan` or `kmitl.project` could own (ADR-0004, ADR-0007). Consuming documents (`purchase.request` พ.1 and `approval.request`) gain a **draw-down mode**: selecting an existing commitment (`budget_commitment_id`) makes them link-and-obligate/consume against it instead of reserving their own — **the presence of a picked commitment IS the discriminator, no new flag**. This inverts the old "select dimensions → reserve" flow into an optional "reserve first → pick up later," so a unit can spend a reservation without jumping into the plan/project modules to create the พ.1.

## Considered Options

- **Model a separate "budget support request" parent** that owns the commitment — rejected: reintroduces the parent model the standalone concept was meant to remove; the reservation slip itself is the artifact, and any future request document can *feed* its creation without being its structural parent.
- **Limit the picker to standalone slips only** — rejected: plan/project พ.1 would still require module-hopping, missing the point of the feature.

## Consequences

- The capability lives on the shared `budget.commitment.mixin`, so `purchase.request` and `approval.request` behave identically (both are named in the original problem as select-then-reserve implementations).
- A drawn document **never re-reserves** and inherits the commitment's `analytic_distribution` + fiscal year **locked** (same server-side lock as project พ.1, ADR-0007), so spend can never diverge from the reservation.
- **Cardinality is preserved per source**: plan keeps one-active-PR (ADR-0006 — the picker excludes plan commitments that already have an active PR); project and **standalone** allow many drawing documents, capped at the commitment amount, with the ledger `_check_commitment_limits` as the backstop. The picker is a *new entry point*, not a change to existing invariants.
- **Lifecycle stays with the reservation's owner**: cancel (keep-if-touched, per the plan/project `_release` rule — a touched commitment is kept for audit, never stranded) and return-unused (คืนจอง, ADR-0009) are owner actions; a beneficiary spends but does not cancel/return. Draw-down itself is open to anyone who can *see* the slip (KISS). `operating_unit_id` and `beneficiary_operating_unit_id` are editable only in `draft`.
- **Phasing**: phase 1 exposes `budget_commitment_id` as a filtered dropdown (`state in (reserved, partial)`, `available_to_obligate > 0`, matching fiscal year, visible to the user); phase 2 unifies selection into the existing reservation picker **widget**, where one "เลือก/จองงบประมาณ" action lets the user either pick an existing slip or fall through to the reserve-new flow.
- Cross-OU access and accounting of a picked slip are covered by [ADR-0011](./0011-cross-ou-reservation-beneficiary-unit.md).

### Refinements from the first UAT round

- **A reservation is identified by what it is for, not by its number.** `budget.commitment` gains `title` (ชื่อรายการจอง) and shows `BC0001 - <title>` as its `display_name`, because a bare `BC0001` in the draw-down dropdown gave the user nothing to choose on. Reservations created from a source document inherit the source's own human name — `procurement.plan.description` (ชื่อรายการ, its `_rec_name`; `name` is the document code) and `kmitl.project.name` — so plan/project slips are self-labelling and only a standalone slip needs typing. `ref` is left alone: it keeps holding the source's *code* (`plan.name`, `project.key`) and stays a search key, so the two are not conflated.
- **Picking is separated from drawing.** The reservation may be selected from `draft` — the beneficiary who was given a slip names it while writing the request — while the draw-down itself still happens at the budget step (`approval.request` `to_verify` → `_action_draw_from_reservation` → `action_submit`; `purchase.request` at `to_approve`). Previously the field was gated on `hide_reserve_budget_button`, i.e. only at the budget step, which contradicted the blank-slate chart picker being available from `draft` and left the requester unable to state which slip they were spending.
- **Both pickers are gated by `is_budget_editable`,** which means "budget selection is still open on this request" and is therefore also exactly when a reservation may be picked. Enumerating states in the view is not viable: `purchase.request` draws its states from four modules (`to_verify` comes from `purchase_request_approval_kmitl`, `to_submit`/`cancelled` from `purchase_request_kmitl`, `to_examine` from `purchase_request_verify_state`) and three of them override this compute — a hand-written list silently missed `to_verify`, reintroducing the very inconsistency above. Picking a reservation therefore no longer forces `is_budget_editable` false (the user must be able to un-pick); the chart picker is hidden view-side while a reservation is picked, since dimensions then come from the slip.
- **The document's own dimension fields are hidden once a commitment exists** — the card above already lists them, and before reservation they are still shown because they are how the chart picker's choice is confirmed. Safe against the invisible-but-required trap: `_checkValidity` flags a `many2one` only when its value is *empty*, and the fields are hidden exactly when a commitment exists, which is only ever true after they were written.
- **The widget lists โครงการ/กิจกรรม and แผนจัดซื้อจัดจ้าง only when set.** They are source markers rather than part of every reservation — a standalone slip carries neither — so listing them unconditionally would add two empty rows to the common case. The four financial dimensions are listed even when empty, because the engine matches all dimensions or pins a missing one to `False` (`budget.controller`), so an absent กองทุน is information, not noise. They are reachable at all because the widget also sits on `budget_commitment_id`, where a plan/project พ.1 shows its shared commitment.
- **The slip shows its own details inline** via the `budget_commitment_info` field widget — on `reservation_commitment_id` while picking *and* on `budget_commitment_id` once reserved, so a reservation this document made for itself and one it drew from someone else read identically (the AR's ad-hoc "Budget Reserved: BC0001 — amount" alert is replaced by it; the card's state badge carries the reserved cue) (extends `Many2OneField`, so selection is unchanged and the panel is appended underneath): budget code, all four financial dimensions, fiscal year and วงเงินอนุมัติ / ผูกพันแล้ว / คงเหลือให้ผูกพัน. The payload comes from `budget.commitment.get_reservation_info()` as label/value rows, so labels, translations and money formatting stay server-side, and `_reservation_info_rows` is left as the extension point should a bridge ever need to add its own. The owning/beneficiary units are deliberately **not** shown: the OU is the operator/visibility axis (ADR-0011), not something the person picking a slip decides by, and repeating it here only crowded out the dimensions that do matter. This delivers phase 2's *"see what you are picking"* goal without merging the two pickers into one action; that merge remains open.
- **Known gap.** Reading `budget.commitment` requires `group_budget_viewer` (which implies `base.group_user`), so a requester without a budget role sees an empty draw-down dropdown even though the global OU rules would already limit the rows to their own unit's or beneficiary slips. Whether to grant `base.group_user` read on `budget.commitment` or to give requesters the viewer role is a deployment decision, deliberately not taken here.
