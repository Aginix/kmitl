Adds **เอกสารอ้างอิงเพิ่มเติม** to the loan form: any number of further
documents cited beside the source document — e.g. a หนังสือสารบรรณ backing
a loan for a purchase request (advance_payment ADR-0029).

The generic mechanism (kinds, lookup, snapshot, ถูกอ้างอิงโดย) is
``agx_related_document``; this module only puts it on the loan and keeps
the loan's own rules. The rows are evidence only: they never drive the
borrowing headroom, the dimensions or the route.

Who may change them, until the agreement is closed or cancelled:

- the borrower may add rows at any time, but remove only the rows they
  added, and only while the request is a draft;
- unit finance (``group_advance_payment_user``) and the loan officer may
  add and remove any row.

Every addition and removal is logged on the agreement. An endorser only
reads.

``agx_related_document_sarabun`` seeds the หนังสือสารบรรณ kind.
