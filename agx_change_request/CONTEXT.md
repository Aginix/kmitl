# Change Requests

Lets whoever checks a record (ผู้ตรวจสอบ) ask its maker to fix or explain something, and
follow each request until its author is satisfied — on the record itself or pinned on one
of its files. Only records whose module takes Change requests have them.

## Language

**Change request (ข้อแก้ไข)**: A request on a record, made by anyone who can read it,
asking the maker to fix or explain something. Not made by default: most of what a
reviewer writes or draws is just the trace of their review, and only Change requests are
followed up. Every step of one — made, edited, assigned, answered, closed, reopened,
cancelled, deleted — is posted on the record's chatter as it happens. Outstanding ones
never hold up the record by themselves; the record's own module may refuse a step while
some are outstanding. _Avoid_: issue, task, open comment

**Pinned Change request**: A Change request tied to a point of a page of one Revision of
a file attached to the record; its pin is a Comment of that file's Annotation layer (see
Photo Viewer — Annotation). Never turns into a General one. _Avoid_: comment request

**General Change request**: A Change request on the record only, with no pin. Never
turns into a Pinned one. _Avoid_: record comment, note

**Open / Answered / Closed**: A Change request is **open** when made or reopened,
**answered** once someone other than its author Replies — and open again if its author
Replies to it then (e.g. "still missing") — and **closed** by its
author — who may reopen it instead — or on the author's behalf by an administrator or by
whoever the record's module allows (e.g. a Buddy covering a budget transfer). A request
is **outstanding** while open or answered, whoever made it; a module that refuses a step
while some are outstanding counts them all. _Avoid_: resolved, done, fixed, pending

**Cancelled**: The end of a Pinned Change request whose file was deleted. It keeps its
text and the name of that file and is never reopened; it is not Closed, since nobody
said the fix was made. _Avoid_: closed by the system, orphaned

**Edited**: A Change request whose author changed it — allowed at any time, answered or
not. It shows that it was edited and keeps its earlier texts; a deleted one leaves its
text in the record's chatter. _Avoid_: amended, revised (Revision is a file's)

**Reply (การตอบ)**: One message of the conversation on a Change request — "fixed in
revision 2", "line 3 is still wrong" — by anyone who can read the record, its author
included. Replies follow one another and are never changed; each is also posted on the
record's chatter. Only an open or answered request is replied to. _Avoid_: response
(one text replacing the last), comment (a Comment is a mark on a file)

**Assignee (ผู้รับมอบหมาย)**: A user a Change request is assigned to, chosen by its author;
a request has none, one or several. Assigning makes them a follower of the record. Each Assignee has an activity on the record to answer
it while the request is open, cleared once it is answered or closed and given back when
it is reopened. Answering is not reserved to Assignees. Its author has an activity to
check the answer once someone else Replies, cleared once they reply or it is closed.
_Avoid_: recipient,
responsible, owner
