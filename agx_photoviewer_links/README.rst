=====================
Photo Viewer - Links
=====================

Opens link attachments (``web_attachment_link``) in the photo viewer, next to
the files of the same list:

* YouTube videos play in the viewer.
* Google Drive files, Docs, Sheets, Slides and folders are embedded through
  Google's preview; what shows depends on the viewer's own Google session.
* A link to a record or an action of this Odoo shows a card naming the
  document — read with the user's own rights — with buttons to open it here
  or in a new tab.
* Any other link shows a card with a button to open it in a new tab.

The Download button becomes *Open at origin* for links. Installed
automatically with ``agx_photoviewer`` and ``web_attachment_link``.
